隐私Privacy

写实话,不写律师话。这一页说清楚哪些数据存在、存在哪里、谁看得见。 Written plainly, not defensively. What data exists, where it sits, and who can read it.

你和 agent 之间传的东西What travels between you and the agent

命令、文件内容、截屏、剪贴板 —— 这些在离开你的 Mac 之前就已经加密(X25519 密钥协商 + ChaCha20-Poly1305)。密钥只存在于你的 Mac 和你的 agent 两端。中转服务器负责转发密文,解不开,也不保存内容。 Commands, file contents, screenshots, clipboard text — all encrypted before they leave your Mac (X25519 key agreement plus ChaCha20-Poly1305). The keys exist only on your Mac and in your agent. The relay forwards ciphertext; it cannot read it and does not store it.

中转服务器确实会记的东西What the relay does record

为了把消息投递到正确的一端并排查故障,服务器会记录连接层面的元数据:设备与 agent 的公钥标识、连接与断开的时间、来源 IP。不含命令内容、文件内容、截屏。 To route messages and debug outages the server keeps connection metadata: the public-key identifiers of the device and agent, connect and disconnect timestamps, and the source IP address. It does not include command contents, file contents or screenshots.

不想经过我们的服务器,可以自己架一台中转服务器,把 App 指过去。 If you'd rather not pass through ours at all, run your own relay and point the app at it.

审计日志只在你自己机器上The audit log never leaves your Mac

每条请求、你的决定、执行结果都写在 ~/Library/Logs/MacHands/audit.log。这个文件不会上传,我们看不到。你可以随时删。 Every request, your decision and the outcome go to ~/Library/Logs/MacHands/audit.log. That file is never uploaded and we never see it. Delete it whenever you like.

购买时的信息When you buy

付款由第三方支付服务商处理,我们拿不到你的卡号。我们保存的是签发和补发许可证所必需的最少信息:邮箱、订单号、购买时间。 Payment is handled by a third-party provider; we never see your card number. We keep only what's needed to issue and re-issue a license: your email address, the order id and the purchase date.

你可以要求删除Deletion

写信给我们要求删除购买记录即可,处理后你的许可证仍然有效,但我们将无法再为你补发。 Ask us and we'll delete your purchase record. Your license keeps working afterwards, but we will no longer be able to re-issue it for you.
