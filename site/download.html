配对成功后窗口会自己弹出来。点一下就生效,自检自己跑完,之后不再打扰。The window opens itself once paired. Your click takes effect immediately, the self-check runs on its own, and it won't ask again.

它会要三项系统权限It asks for three system permissions

  • 通知Notifications — 命令跑完或需要你决定时提醒你一声。so it can tell you a job finished or needs you.
  • 屏幕录制Screen Recording — 截屏和录屏。for screenshots and screen recordings.
  • 辅助功能Accessibility — 控制键盘鼠标。to drive the keyboard and mouse.

三项都能在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里随时收回。不给也能用,只是对应的能力不可用,自检会如实标红。 All three are revocable in System Settings → Privacy & Security. MacHands still runs without them — the matching abilities just go red in the self-check.
